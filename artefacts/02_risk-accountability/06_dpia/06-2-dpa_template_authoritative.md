## Template
This DPA template governs the processing of Digital Personal Data by a Data Processor on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.

Rule: No third-party processing is lawful without a signed DPA aligned with this template. [DPDP-S7-Rule-2]

### 1. Purpose of This Agreement
This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) governs processing by a Data Processor on behalf of the Data Fiduciary in compliance with the DPDP Act.

### 2. Parties
Data Fiduciary: [Legal Name], [Address]

Data Processor: [Legal Name], [Address]

### 3. Definitions
Terms not defined herein have meanings under the DPDP Act.

### 4. Scope & Purpose Limitation
4.1 Processor processes only on documented instructions and for purposes in Annex A. [DPDP-S8-Rule-1]

4.2 Processing beyond documented purpose is prohibited.

4.3 Processor shall not infer, extend, or reinterpret consent.

### 5. Processor Obligations
Processor SHALL implement technical and organizational safeguards, confidentiality, records, and assistance.

Processor SHALL NOT use data for its own purposes, combine data across clients, infer consent, or retain beyond instructed duration.

### 6. Consent & Legal Basis
- Processing must be backed by valid consent or lawful basis.
- Withdrawal must be honored immediately upon instruction. [DPDP-S6-Rule-4]

### 7. Sub-Processing
Requires prior written approval; sub-processors bound to no less strict obligations.

### 8. Security Measures
Implement access controls, encryption, audit logging, breach detection. [DPDP-S10-Rule-1]

### 9. Audit & Accountability
Provide evidence; audits/inspections; logs should be immutable and complete.

### 10. Breach Notification
Report breaches without undue delay; include nature, categories, mitigation.

### 11. Data Retention & Deletion
Retain only as instructed; deletion must be provable.

### 12. Cross-Border Transfers
Require explicit authorization and DPDP compliance.

### 13. Indemnity & Liability
Processor indemnifies for violations and damages arising from processor fault.

### 14. Term & Termination
Cease processing on termination; return or delete data.

### 15. Governing Law
India.

### Annex A — Processing Details
- Nature
- Purpose
- Data categories
- Duration

### Annex B — Security Controls
- Technical safeguards
- Organizational safeguards
